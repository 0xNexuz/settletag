SHEET 04 / TESTNET FUNDING

Fund the test■

The official Mezo faucet issues test BTC and MEZO. MUSD is minted by borrowing against test BTC.

01

Copy wallet address

Create or unlock a test wallet first.

02

Request test BTC

Paste the address into the official faucet and complete its CAPTCHA. The faucet also offers test MEZO.

Open official faucet ↗
03

Get test MUSD

Connect a compatible wallet to Mezo testnet and borrow MUSD against test BTC in the Mezo app. The faucet does not dispense MUSD.

Open MUSD borrow ↗

The embedded test wallet can send tokens in SettleTag. Mezo’s borrow app may require a browser wallet extension. For borrowing, using a separate extension account is simpler than importing this wallet’s recovery phrase.
