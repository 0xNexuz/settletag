SHEET 05 / TESTNET FUNDING

Get test BTC■

Mezo's faucet dispenses test BTC. With BTC you can borrow MUSD inside SettleTag, or swap a little BTC for MUSD in the Mezo app.

01

Copy wallet address

Create or unlock a test wallet first.

02

Request test BTC

Paste the address into the official faucet and complete its CAPTCHA. Keep some test BTC for transaction fees.

Open official faucet ↗
03

Borrow MUSD here

Open a trove on the Trove sheet, or borrow while paying an invoice. Mezo's minimum loan is 1,800 MUSD, so a new trove needs enough BTC collateral to stay at 150% or more.

04

Or swap a small amount

Connect a browser wallet to Mezo testnet. In the Mezo app, open Swap and choose BTC → MUSD. Review the live quote, fees, and slippage before confirming.

Open Mezo testnet ↗Read Mezo Swap guide ↗

The SettleTag wallet cannot connect to Mezo Swap directly. To swap, send test BTC to an extension wallet from the Wallet sheet, swap there, then send MUSD back. Keep your recovery phrase private.
